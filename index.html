Challenge format

Visualize one dataset. Explain what it reveals.

Participants receive one week to visualize one of two scientific datasets. The final submission is a single image with a caption that explains the insight. Supporting process notes, failed attempts, and analysis details are welcome when they sharpen the scientific story.
